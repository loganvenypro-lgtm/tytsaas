Trending charts mostly show channels that are already huge. What matters when you're looking for an idea is a video that **far exceeds its own channel's norm**: that's what we call an outlier.

## The multiplier, not the views

A 2-million-view video on a channel averaging 3 million is a flop. The same video on a channel averaging 80,000 is a huge signal: the topic, title or thumbnail hooked far beyond the usual audience.

| Channel | Average | Video | Multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Big channel | 3M | 2M | 0.7x |
| Small channel | 80k | 2M | 25x |

<Callout tone="tip">
  An outlier on a **small** channel is the most useful: it proves the idea works without the help of a big audience.
</Callout>

## What to take from it

1. **The topic**: does it come back in other outliers of the niche?
2. **The title format**: question, number, contrast, challenge…
3. **The thumbnail**: what it promises in half a second.
