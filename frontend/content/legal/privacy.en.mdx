## Returbe uses YouTube API Services

Returbe uses **YouTube API Services** (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) to show video and channel statistics. By using Returbe, you agree to be bound by the <a href={YOUTUBE_TERMS_URL}>YouTube Terms of Service</a>. Data obtained through these services is also subject to the <a href={GOOGLE_PRIVACY_URL}>Google Privacy Policy</a>.

## Data we collect

### Your account
- Your name, email address and profile picture, given at sign-up or by Google or Discord if you sign in with them.
- Your password, only in hashed form if you sign up with an email.
- Your settings: language, niche, folders, followed channels, alerts and recap preferences.

### Public YouTube data
Returbe indexes **public** YouTube data through the YouTube Data API: video titles, thumbnails, view, like and comment counts, publish dates, channel names and subscriber counts. This data is not about your account.

### Your YouTube channel (only if you connect it)
If you choose to connect your channel in "My channel", you authorize Returbe through your Google account to:
- **youtube.readonly**: know which channel is yours and read its videos;
- **yt-analytics.readonly**: read your channel's YouTube Analytics statistics (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate).

Returbe **never** has write access to your YouTube account: it cannot publish, edit or delete anything.

### Site usage
- Anonymized usage statistics (page views, clicks) through PostHog, to improve the product.
- The page you came from when you signed up (for example a shared link).
- The messages you send us through the Feedback form.

## How we use this data
- To run the service: your account, searches, alerts and recap.
- To show your channel's statistics to you only (and to your team members if you invite them).
- To send account emails: welcome, new sign-in, password reset, and the recap if you turned it on.
- To understand how the product is used, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with other users.

## Who we share data with
Only with the providers that run the service, and only for that:
- **Google** (sign-in and YouTube API Services);
- **Discord** (sign-in if you choose it, and internal notifications to the Returbe team);
- **Resend** (sending emails);
- **PostHog** (usage statistics);
- our hosting provider and database.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

## Cookies and storage on your device
Returbe sets cookies and uses your browser's local storage to:
- keep you signed in (session cookie, required);
- remember your language and display preferences;
- measure site usage (PostHog).

## How long we keep data
- Account data is kept while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of its deletion.
- Your channel's data obtained through YouTube API Services is refreshed regularly and deleted at most **30 days** after you revoke access or delete your account.
- Indexed public YouTube data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.

## Revoking access and deleting your data
- **Disconnect YouTube**: in Settings → Account, the "Disconnect YouTube" button immediately revokes Returbe's access to your Google account and deletes your channel's private statistics. You can also revoke access from your <a href={GOOGLE_PERMISSIONS_URL}>Google account permissions page</a>.
- **Delete your account**: in Settings → Account, the "Delete my account" button erases your account and all associated data, immediately. You can also email us at <a href={`mailto:${LEGAL.email}`}>{LEGAL.email}</a>.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR, you can access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and ask for a copy of it. Email us at <a href={`mailto:${LEGAL.email}`}>{LEGAL.email}</a>. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

## Security
Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed and Google access tokens are stored server-side, never in your browser.
